Sofle carrying cases

Three print-ready cases for the Sofle-J Choc halves, developed from the five concepts below. Each is modelled around PandaKB's newer case and your measured 18 mm keycap height, checked for fit against the keyboard, and exported as STL and 3MF files for an MJF nylon print service.

Light grey is the keyboard, teal is printed nylon (MJF PA12).

Design 1

Spacer ring

PA12 · 45 g151 × 125 × 38 mm1 part + 2 bands

The ring rests on both rims and keeps the keycap tops 2 mm apart. Skirts 4 mm deep, with 0.5 mm clearance, stop the halves sliding. A notch steps it over the 14 mm display column on the inner edge. Two pairs of ridges keep the silicone bands in place.

Design 4

Snap-on lids

PA12 · 2 × 51 g150 × 125 × 23 mm per half2 parts, mirror images

The lid's underside sits 2 mm above the keycaps. The 2.2 mm top is stiff enough that a few kilograms of backpack load won't push it onto the keys. An inner ledge rests on the rim and on the display column. The skirt covers the whole case wall, and four flexible tabs click a small bead under the case's 0.45 mm bottom chamfer.

Design 5

Hinged clamshell

PA12 · 80 g162 × 125 × 41 mm2 trays + pin + 4 magnets

Each half clicks into its own tray, where three tabs catch the 1 mm chamfer on its rim. The trays turn on a 2 mm steel pin through seven knuckles along the inner edge and close with two pairs of 8 × 2 mm magnets on the pinky side. Thumb lips at the front help pull it open. Opened flat, the halves lie side by side, keys up.

Ordering

  • Print in MJF PA12 nylon (grey, or dyed black to hide scuffs) at JLC3DP, PCBWay or Craftcloud. Upload the STL files for an instant quote; no supports or settings are needed.
  • Clamshell hardware: four 8 × 2 mm N52 disc magnets, glued in pairs that attract, and a 2 mm stainless or carbon-fibre rod cut to 84 mm for the hinge.
  • Spacer ring: two flat silicone bands about 12 mm wide, with a relaxed circumference of 230–260 mm.
  • Fits allow 0.5 mm clearance to the case walls, and the click beads overlap the case's chamfers by 0.35–0.5 mm. For an extra check before a full order, a small test piece with one tab can be printed first.
